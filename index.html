Kaalchakra Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026 · App: Kaalchakra (कालचक्र), package com.kaalchakracalendar.app

Kaalchakra is an Indian calendar (panchang) in Marathi, Hindi, English, Telugu, Kannada and Gujarati. It is built to work entirely on your phone.

The short version

  • We collect nothing. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • The app has no internet access. It does not request Android's internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
  • Your location never leaves your phone, and is only used if you ask for it.

All calculations (tithi, nakshatra, sunrise, festivals) are done on the device.

Data on your phone

The app saves your settings (language, calendar tradition, city, theme, alert times) and the reminders you create, only on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

If Android's own backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include these settings and reminders in your personal Google backup, as it does for most apps. Kaalchakra itself never receives or reads that backup.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
